# Nimbus University Hostel Rules (mock document)

_Mock content written for Assignment 8. It is not a real university's policy._

1. **In-time:** Residents must be inside the hostel by 22:00 on weekdays and 23:00 on Saturdays.
   Late entry needs a gate pass approved by the warden.
2. **Visitors:** Visitors are allowed in the ground-floor lounge from 10:00 to 19:00 only, and must sign the register.
3. **Quiet hours:** 23:00 to 06:00. No music without headphones.
4. **Appliances:** Electric kettles and irons only. Heaters, induction cooktops and extension boards above 6 A are not allowed.
5. **Mess:** Breakfast 07:30–09:00, lunch 12:30–14:00, dinner 19:30–21:30. Mess leave needs 24 hours' notice.
6. **Room changes:** Apply to the warden's office in the first two weeks of a semester.
7. **Ragging:** Zero tolerance. Report incidents to the anti-ragging helpline or the warden immediately.
8. **Damage:** Residents pay for damage to hostel property in their room.
